How it works

  1. Allow accessGive Inreel access to your Photos. No sign-up, no account.
  2. Swipe upEach swipe brings a random photo or video, full-screen. Swipe down to go back.
  3. Keep or tossHeart the ones you love. Swipe left on the ones you don't.

Rediscover what you forgot you had

Thousands of photos sit untouched in your library. Inreel shuffles them back into view — the trip from three years ago, the video you never rewatched.

  • Heart your favorites and find them all together in the Liked tab.
  • See when, where and on which device it was taken — and which album it's in.
  • Long videos get a progress bar you can scrub.
  • Share anything with the standard iOS share sheet.

Reel 02

Clean up as you go

Rediscovering old photos is also the best moment to let go of the ones you don't need. Blurry shots, duplicates, screenshots from 2019 — toss them as you scroll.

  • Swipe left or tap the trash icon to drop an item into the pending basket.
  • Changed your mind? Just undo.
  • Nothing is deleted until you confirm once in the iOS system dialog.
  • As always on iOS, deleted items go to Recently Deleted first.

Your photos never leave your phone

Inreel has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers to send anything to. The app itself makes no network requests.

  • No account or sign-up
  • No uploads, no cloud sync of its own
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No AI analysis of your photos

If some originals are stored in iCloud, iOS may download them so they can be played — that's handled by the system, not Inreel.

FAQ

Is Inreel really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no ads, no account. It's free, and it'll stay that way.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Inreel doesn't upload anything, and the app itself makes no network requests. If an original lives in iCloud, iOS may download it to your phone so it can be shown.

Can I delete photos by accident?

No. Swiping left only moves an item into the pending basket, and you can undo it. Photos are deleted only after you confirm in the iOS system dialog.

Does it use AI or decide what I see?

No. There's no AI and no recommendation algorithm. Inreel simply shows your photos and videos in random order.

What do I need to use it?

A device running iOS 17 or later, and permission for Inreel to access your Photos.
